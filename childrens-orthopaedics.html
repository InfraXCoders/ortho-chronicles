Overview

Children are not small adults: their bones have growth plates and they remodel after injury, so treatment differs. Most birth deformities such as clubfoot are highly treatable if started early, and many limps are harmless — but a limp with fever, night pain or refusal to bear weight needs urgent assessment.

When to seek urgent care

  • A child who will not use a limb or bear weight
  • Fever with a limp or a swollen joint
  • A curve of the spine or rapidly worsening deformity
