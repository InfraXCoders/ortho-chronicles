Overview

Sprains, strains, tendon overuse and ligament tears are common in athletes of all levels. First aid (POLICE), early guided movement and sport-specific rehabilitation return most people to play. A structured return-to-sport pathway reduces re-injury.

When to seek urgent care

  • A pop with immediate swelling or instability
  • Inability to bear weight or move a joint
  • Pain that persists beyond 6 weeks despite rest
