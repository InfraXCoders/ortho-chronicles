Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how OrthoChronicles.info ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website. OrthoChronicles is an educational platform for orthopedic knowledge, supervised by Dr. Maninder Singh, MS Orthopedics, Associate Professor, Government Medical College & Hospital, Amritsar.

Information We Collect

We do not require registration to read our content. We may collect:

  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, browser type, device type and approximate location (country/city level), collected via Google Analytics.
  • Contact information — only if you voluntarily contact us by phone, WhatsApp or email, or submit an article for publication.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored in your browser — for the following purposes:

  • Analytics cookies: Google Analytics uses cookies to help us understand how visitors use the site, so we can improve our content.
  • Advertising cookies: We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners use advertising cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

You can disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to read our content.

Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We display ads served by Google AdSense. Please note:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to measure website traffic. The data is aggregated and anonymous — we cannot identify individual visitors. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Medical Information Disclaimer

Content on OrthoChronicles is for educational and informational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. We do not collect any patient health information through this website. Always consult a qualified physician regarding any medical condition. See our full Terms of Use & Medical Disclaimer.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to inquiries and article submissions
  • To display relevant advertising that supports free access to our content
