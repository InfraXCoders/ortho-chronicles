Overview

Most broken bones heal well with the right alignment, stable fixation and time. The first hours matter: control bleeding, immobilise the limb, give pain relief and reach a trauma-capable hospital. Healing depends on the bone, blood supply, age, nutrition and smoking.

When to seek urgent care

  • Open wound over a fracture or a bone visible through the skin
  • Pale, cold or numb limb, or severe pain not controlled by a splint
  • Suspected spine, pelvis or hip fracture — do not move the person
