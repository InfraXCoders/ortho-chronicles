How to use / shortcuts
Tools: V move · L label · A arrow · I line · B pen · H highlighter · T text · M measure.

Labels: pick Label, click the structure, then type in the box that appears. Double-click a label to re-edit it; drag the box to reposition the text while the pointer stays on the structure.

Edit: click any mark to select it — drag to move, drag its ends to reshape, change colour/size/opacity above, or delete it.

View: scroll or +/− to zoom, Space+drag or the Pan tool to move, Fit to reset.

Note: sample studies are real, de-identified scans from Wikimedia Commons (open licences — credit shown on each image). Upload your own DICOM/JPEG for private cases.
