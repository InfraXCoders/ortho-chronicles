Overview

We use our hands for everything, so small problems become big annoyances. Numbness and tingling at night usually points to nerve compression at the wrist (carpal tunnel) or elbow; pain on the outer elbow with gripping suggests tennis elbow; locking fingers, thumb-base arthritis and wrist fractures after a fall are other common problems. Early splinting, activity modification and exercise solve many cases.

When to seek urgent care

  • Constant numbness or thumb muscle wasting
  • Deformity or severe swelling after a fall (possible fracture)
  • Fever, redness or swelling in a finger or hand (possible infection)
