Overview

The knee takes more load than any other joint. Wear-and-tear arthritis, ligament injuries (ACL, MCL), meniscus tears and kneecap pain account for most visits. Treatment starts with exercise, weight control and sensible activity changes; injections, arthroscopy and replacement are used selectively.

When to seek urgent care

  • A locked knee that will not straighten
  • A hot, red, swollen knee with fever
  • A knee giving way after a twisting injury with swelling
