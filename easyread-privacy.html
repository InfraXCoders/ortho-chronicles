Easy Read — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Easy Read ("the app") is a PDF and EPUB reader published by InfraXCoders / OrthoChronicles. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What stays on your device

The books you open, your highlights, notes, drawings, bookmarks, reading position and settings are stored only on your device, inside the app. We do not have access to them, we do not upload them, and the app has no user accounts.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or sell personal information, and the app has no analytics. The app shows ads served by Google AdMob (see below).

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows a banner on the library screen and an occasional full-screen ad after you close a book. Ads are never shown while you read. Google AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and similar technical data (such as IP address and device type) to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In regions that require it (EEA, UK), the app asks for your consent first, and you can change it later from the library menu (Ad privacy settings). You can also reset or opt out of your advertising ID in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. The content of your books is never shared with advertisers.

Dictionary, simpler words and translation

When you tap Define, only the word or short passage you selected is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to third-party services to return results: dictionaryapi.dev (definitions), Datamuse (synonyms) and MyMemory (translation). No other part of your book and no identifier of yours is sent by the app. These services have their own privacy policies. If you do not use this feature, nothing is sent.

Files and permissions

The app reads a PDF or EPUB only when you choose it, or open it with "Open with Easy Read". It does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or storage permissions. It declares the Android advertising ID permission for ads. Backups and exported notes are created only when you ask and are saved or shared where you choose.

Children

The app is a general-purpose reading tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Remove books inside the app, or uninstall the app, to delete all data it stores on the device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated date.
