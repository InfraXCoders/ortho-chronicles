About the Doctor

Dr. Maninder Singh

MS Orthopedics  |  Associate Professor  |  Trauma & Joint Replacement Surgeon

Dr. Maninder Singh is a distinguished orthopedic surgeon and Associate Professor at the prestigious Government Medical College and Hospital, Amritsar. With over 15 years of expertise in Trauma Surgery and Joint Replacement, he has transformed the lives of thousands of patients across Punjab and beyond.

Practicing at Guru Nanak Dev Hospital, one of the largest teaching hospitals in North India, Dr. Singh combines cutting-edge surgical techniques with compassionate patient care. His expertise spans complex fracture management, total knee replacement, hip replacement, and sports injuries.

What We Treat

Specialized Services

Comprehensive orthopedic care with the latest surgical techniques and technology

Total Knee Replacement

Advanced knee arthroplasty for arthritis and degenerative joint disease, restoring pain-free movement and mobility.

Hip Replacement

Total and partial hip replacement surgeries using the latest implants for long-lasting, natural joint function.

Trauma Surgery

Expert management of complex fractures, road traffic accidents, and polytrauma with modern fixation techniques.

Sports Injuries

Ligament repairs, ACL reconstruction, meniscus surgery, and shoulder stabilization for athletes of all levels.

Spine Disorders

Diagnosis and surgical management of disc herniation, spinal stenosis, and vertebral fractures.

Shoulder & Elbow

Rotator cuff repairs, shoulder replacement, elbow arthroscopy, and tendon reconstruction procedures.

Pediatric Orthopedics

Treatment of congenital deformities, growth-related conditions, and fractures in children and adolescents.

Revision Surgery

Complex revision arthroplasty for failed implants, infections, and instability after previous joint surgeries.
