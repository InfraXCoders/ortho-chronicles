Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how OrthoChronicles ("we", "us") handles information when you use the OrthoChronicles Calorie Tracker app ("App") and website (orthochronicles.info).

1. Information We Collect

The App collects the following information only when you choose to provide it:

  • Profile data — age, weight, height, gender, activity level, and dietary goal entered during onboarding. This is used solely to calculate your daily calorie and macro targets.
  • Food and meal logs — items you add to your daily log. Stored locally on your device and, if you sign in with Google, synced to your personal Firebase Firestore account.
  • Water intake — daily water tracking entries.
  • Body weight history — optional weight entries for progress tracking.
  • Google account information — if you choose to sign in with Google, we store your display name, email address, and profile photo URL to identify your cloud-sync account. We never access your Google contacts, messages, or any other Google data.
  • Consultation queries — if you use the "Consult an Expert" feature, your typed message is sent directly to us via your device's email app. We do not intercept or store it on any server.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate and display your personalised calorie and macro targets.
  • To sync your food log and profile across devices when you are signed in with Google.
  • To respond to consultation queries sent via email.
  • We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.

3. Data Storage

  • Local storage — all data is primarily stored on your device using browser localStorage (website) or Android SharedPreferences / localStorage (app). Uninstalling the app removes all local data.
  • Cloud storage — if you sign in with Google, data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore under your unique user ID. This data is protected by Google's security infrastructure. You can delete your cloud data at any time by signing out and contacting us.

4. Third-Party Services

The App and website use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore) — for optional Google Sign-In and cloud data sync. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts — to load the Poppins and Playfair Display typefaces. No personal data is sent.
  • Google AdSense — the website displays ads. Google may use cookies to personalise ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics (Firebase) — anonymous usage analytics to improve the App. No personally identifiable information is collected.

5. Permissions

The Android app requests the following device permissions:

  • CAMERA — used only for the barcode scanner feature to identify food items. Camera feed is processed on-device and never stored or transmitted.
  • INTERNET — required to load the tracker, sync data, and display Google Sign-In.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to detect whether the device is online or offline.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Local data — use "Reset Profile" in the App drawer, or clear app data from Android Settings.
  • Cloud data — contact us at gaganrai5523@gmail.com with the subject "Delete My Data" and we will remove your Firestore data within 7 days.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
