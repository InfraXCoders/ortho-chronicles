How to Delete Your Data

The Calorie & Nutrition Tracker app stores all data locally on your device only. No account is created and no data is sent to any server.

To permanently delete all app data:
  1. Open Settings on your Android device
  2. Go to Apps (or Application Manager)
  3. Find Calorie Tracker
  4. Tap Storage
  5. Tap Clear Data

This will permanently delete all your meals, goals, and history from the device.

Note: Uninstalling the app also removes all stored data from your device.

No Account to Delete

This app does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information. There is no server-side account to delete.
