Delete Account & Data

Calorie & Nutrition Tracker — OrthoChronicles

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your profile and personal information
  • All meal logs and calorie history
  • Water intake records
  • Custom food entries
  • Daily goals and preferences

Option 1 — Delete from the App

  1. Open the Calorie Tracker app
  2. Go to Profile or Settings
  3. Tap Account
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm deletion when prompted

Option 2 — Request via Email

If you are unable to access the app, send a deletion request to:

homenet5523@gmail.com

Include in your email:

  • The email address used to sign in
  • Subject line: "Delete My Account — Calorie Tracker"

We will process your request within 7 business days and send a confirmation email.

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be erased and cannot be recovered.
Note: Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete your account. Please follow the steps above to fully remove your data.
