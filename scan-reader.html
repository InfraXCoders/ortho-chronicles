CT & MRI DICOM (.dcm) 100% on your device

DICOM Scan Reader

Open a CT or MRI scan and read it like a workstation — scroll through the slices, adjust the window/level, zoom, pan, invert and measure. Everything runs inside your browser; your images are never uploaded anywhere.

Private by design. Files are opened locally in your browser and never sent to any server. This is an educational viewer, not a diagnostic device — it does not interpret scans or replace a radiologist's report.

Controls
• Pick a tool above, then drag the image (works with touch)
• Roller / wheel — move through slices
• Pinch — zoom on touch  ·  Right-drag — zoom, Shift+drag — pan (mouse)
• +/− buttons — step zoom, brightness & contrast
• 2-point measure — tap two points for a distance in mm

Supports standard uncompressed and most compressed DICOM (JPEG, JPEG-LS, JPEG 2000, RLE). Some proprietary or heavily-compressed files may not open. This tool is for education and self-study; clinical decisions must rest on a formal radiology report.
